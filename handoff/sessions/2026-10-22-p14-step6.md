# P14 step 6 (zone front end): scope — 2026-10-22

## Does §3.8 still hold after steps 3 to 5?

Yes, at its boundaries. Checked against the code:

- **Copy pointer:** `assemble_side` builds `ENii COPY r` (r ≥ 2 in `init_off`), the `STARTED ON` / `STARTED OFF` links and one `ENii EXHAUSTED` per entry, for `Zone`-only entries too, and returns them in `SideCopies.entries` (`EntryCopies { number, area, mode, exhausted, copies }`; each copy's `shell.start` and `shell.done_hub`). The zone adds only `FIRE` → every `COPY r`, and pushes `START` → `GRANTED`, `DONE HUB` → `WAITING` and `EXHAUSTED` → its own MCUs to `SideLogic.links`.
- **Activate-mode copies (0017):** one stamped copy per run; the zone touches no copy MCU.
- **Spawn distance:** `assemble_side` already places `Zone` and `Both` copies at `plan.zone.spawn_dist_km` (`assemble.rs` ≈164). Step 6 only tests it.
- **The PASS event:** `THsaa INNER` (threshold, step 4) targets `PASS OFF`, `HOT ON`, `COLD OFF DELAY`, `HOLD` and the area's `pass_subscribers`. It fires at the first pass (D = 60 s after a player-country entry) and again at every re-check, H = 120 s apart, while a coalition plane stays inside r_in. So after a cooldown a still-busy area offers the zone a new roll within 2 min, as §3.8 assumes. The threshold runs after the zone, so it reads the zone's subscribers.
- **The clock (0029 B):** `ENii GO` lists copy pointers last run first; every copy's `START` (zone or clock) sets `ENii LAST`, and `DRAW` clears every `LAST` at the next slot. So a zone launch excludes that entry from the next clock slot (B). Step 5 handed over the B / B′ check below.
- **What changes is only the inside of the zone cell:** flight 0 settled J1 (deactivating a running timer cancels it) and J3 (an Activate and a pulse in one tick drop the pulse). Decision 0030 proposes 11 MCUs per zone entry in place of 17.

## The cell as accepted (0030 C; names per §3.0, s = N or D, ii = entry number)

```
 THsaa INNER (PASS) ─► ZNii CHANCE (timer 0.1 s + stagger, Random = chance %)
                    └► ZNii MISS WAIT (timer 1 s + stagger) ─► ZNii COOLDOWN, ZNii DISARM        (the roll failed)
 ZNii CHANCE ─► ZNii FIRE ARM (Activate: WAITING, DENY WAIT)                                     ⟲ re-arm, first
             ├► ZNii DISARM (Deactivate: CHANCE, MISS WAIT)                                       (cancels MISS WAIT, J1)
             └► ZNii FIRE (timer 0.05 s) ─► every ENii COPY r, last run first ─► SNjj START
                                         └► ZNii DENY WAIT (timer 1 s) ─► ZNii WAITING            (refused: U8)
 SNjj START (every copy of entry ii) ─► ZNii GRANTED (Deactivate: DENY WAIT)                       (SideLogic.links)
 SNjj DONE HUB (every copy of entry ii) ─► ZNii WAITING                                            (SideLogic.links)
 ZNii WAITING (relay, starts OFF) ─► ZNii WAITING OFF (Deactivate: WAITING), ZNii COOLDOWN
 ZNii COOLDOWN (timer C = cooldown) ─► ZNii ARM (Activate: CHANCE, MISS WAIT)                      ⟲ re-arm
 ENii EXHAUSTED ─► CHANCE, MISS WAIT, COOLDOWN                                                     (SideLogic.links)
```

- **Armed** = `CHANCE` and `MISS WAIT` on. Both start on. `pass_subscribers[area]` gets both ids.
- **Stagger:** entry p on its area (0-based, in the order `clock::waterfall_order(plan.seed, side, n)` gives the side's n zone entries) adds p·(T_count + 0.2) s, T_count = `budget::recount_time(plan.strategies.census, copies on the side)`.
- **Start states** (`init_off`): `ZNii WAITING`. Nothing in `mission_begin`.
- **Input check (0030 C, item 5):** `build_side` refuses a plan whose hold H (`timing::derived_timing`) is not longer than the longest `MISS WAIT` plus 1 s, and a chance outside 1..=100 % or a cooldown that is not a positive finite number. Plain messages: "Air Tasking NATO zone: …" (`AirSide::label`).
- Same behaviour as §3.8, including its accepted residual (a `Both` entry's clock copy ending first can start the zone's cooldown early).

- **0029's four scenarios (rows 1 to 4) are all within half a launch per mission, on both measures. By 0029's rule B stays.** With the default 3 runs the run cap fills on two entries, and with budget 1 and 20-min sorties B and B′ cannot differ at a slot with room (two different entries cannot both start and end inside one slot interval of at most 26 min).
- **The stress rows (6 runs, 10-min sorties, added by Claude) show the most the rule can matter:** total launches differ by at most +0.26 per mission.
