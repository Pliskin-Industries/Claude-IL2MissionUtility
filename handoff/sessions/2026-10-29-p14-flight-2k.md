# 2026-10-29 — P14 probe flight 2K (what a switched-off running timer does): run sheet

**Status: built, not flown.** Branch `claude/p14-paused-timers`. The user chose to probe before fixing ("probe it first until the root cause is accurately identified") and approved the cells and the order of work in the design session after flight A2.

**Why this flight.** In flight A2 each zone's `MISS WAIT` (1 s) fired 0.88 s after the zone's `ARM` switched it back on. That was 300 s after `DISARM` had switched it off about 0.12 s into its run, so the zone was shut for two cooldowns instead of one. The likely reading is that a running timer that is switched off is **paused**, and switching it on **resumes** it with the time it had left. Flight 0's J1 only showed that a switched-off timer never fires; it was never switched on again. 2K settles this before any fix (decision 0044 will record the fix).

## Cells

All times are mission seconds. Every timer is 10 s at Random 100 unless stated. "OFF" is an `MCU_Deactivate`, "ON" an `MCU_Activate`.

K1 decides between five models of what switching off does to a running timer:

| Cell | Script | Paused, resumes | Restarts on switch-on | Cancelled | Keeps counting while off, output dropped | Ignores the switch |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **K1A** | pulse 60, OFF 64, ON 80 | **86** | 90 | never | never (ends at 70 while off) | 70 |
| **K1B** | pulse 60, OFF 61, ON 100 | **109** | 110 | never | never | 70 |
| **K1C** | pulse 60, OFF 63, ON 71, OFF 73, ON 90 | **95** (7 s left, then 5 s) | 100 | never | never | 70 |

No switch or pulse lands on a timer's natural end (70), so no cell depends on the order within a tick.

K2A and K3A are read once K1A is known:

| Cell | Script | Readings (with K1A = 86) |
|---|---|---|
| **K2A** | pulse 60, OFF 64, ON 80, pulse 82 | **92** if a pulse restarts a resumed timer (flight 0's J5: a pulse restarts a running timer); 86 if it is ignored; 86 and 92 if it runs twice |
| **K3A** | pulse 60, OFF 64, pulse 67 (while off), ON 80 | **86** if the pulse while off is dropped (flight 0's J2 for relays); 90 if it restarted the paused count |

- **K4: a failed random roll on a running timer.** Twenty timers `K4 01` to `K4 20`, Random 50 %, are pulsed at 60 and again at 65, while running.
  - *A failed roll leaves the running timer running:* about a quarter fire at **70** (first roll passed, second failed), about half at 75.
  - *A failed roll stops it, or the roll is made at the end:* **none at 70**, about half at 75.
  - The 75 s readings rely on a passing pulse restarting a running timer, a recorded fact (flight 0, J5).
  - With 20 timers, "none at 70" under the first model has a 0.3 % chance.
  - This decides whether the clock's paused `RANDOM` timers need their own fix.
- **K5: check zones switched off and on.** One AI pair (602) flies in from 20 km south of the cell (flight 2J's KD route). Its entry into the 6 km zones, **E**, is expected between about 150 and 215 s (flight 2J logged 151.98 s on this route); `K5B` gives it. At 90 s the pair is still about 8 km outside; from 250 s it orbits inside until its deletion at 390 s.

  | Zone | Script | Readings |
  |---|---|---|
  | **K5B** (control) | pulse 70 (empty), never switched | fires at **E**: a pulsed zone keeps watching |
  | **K5A** | pulse 70 (empty), OFF 90, ON 300, pulse 330 | fires at **300** if the paused watch resumes, or if switching on alone makes a zone check (K5E tells which); otherwise first at 330 |
  | **K5C** | pulse 330 only | fires at **330**, not at E: a zone watches only after a pulse |
  | **K5D** | pulse 250 (pair inside), OFF 260, ON 300 | fires at 250; fires again at **300** if switching on a zone that already fired makes it check again. That would explain the presence check passing every 160.00 s in A2, not 160.1 |
| **K5E** | never pulsed, OFF 90, ON 300 | fires at **300** only if switching a zone on makes it check with no pulse at all; then K5A at 300 says nothing about a resumed watch |
