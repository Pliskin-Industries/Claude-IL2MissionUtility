### What this settles

- **Root cause of A2's extra cooldown:** a running timer that is switched off is **paused**; switching it on **resumes** it with the time it had left (K1A, K1B, K1C to the tick). Flight 0's J1 ("never fires") is the paused case with no switch-on.
- **Two flight 0 facts hold for a resumed timer too:** a pulse restarts it (K2A), and a pulse while it is off is dropped (K3A).
- **A failed random roll stops a running timer, or the roll is made when it ends** (K4: none at 70 in 20). Either way a stale remainder cannot fire after a failed re-roll. So the clock's `ENii RANDOM` timers, paused by `AREA OUT` and resumed by `RANDOM ON` 0.3 s before `DRAW` re-triggers them, cannot fire out of turn: no fix needed there.
- **Check zones:**
  - a pulse arms a zone, and it stays armed;
  - an armed zone fires when its condition becomes true;
  - switching an armed zone on counts as a new check (K5A, K5D);
  - a zone never pulsed does nothing, on entry or on switch-on (K5C, K5E);
  - a pulse into an armed zone whose condition already holds does nothing (K5A at 330), while a first pulse fires at once (K5C).

  This is why the presence check `THsaa INNER` passes at exactly `HOLD` (160.00 s in A2), not 0.1 s later: `CHECK ON` switches it on, and that is the check. Its `INNER PULSE` is redundant but harmless.
