# Flight 2P: which Complex Trigger event does a kill, a Delete or damage raise? (no pilot)

The trigger has 21 `EventsFilter…` keys and only two event numbers are known: Entered 58 and Left 60. In key order those keys run from 57 (`Spawned`) to 77 (`ParatrooperLandedAlive`), which would put `Killed` at 70, `Damaged` at 67 and `CriticallyDamaged` at 68. That is a guess until flown. Flight names 2N (the two-player flight) and 2O are not used here.

## Reading

- `P1-CT EV 58` three times (about 152 to 215 s) is the control: the trigger works and the stand-ins arrived.
- **The event at 300 s is Killed.** If it is 70, decision 0049 B is built with 70.
- Whatever comes at 360 s is a Delete (2M expects nothing).
- Whatever comes at 420 s is damage without a kill.
- Any other event in the run (for example a "stationary" or "finished" one) is recorded and not acted on.
