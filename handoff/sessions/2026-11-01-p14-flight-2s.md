Every `PCDnn UP k`, `DOWN k`, `CLEAR` and `EMPTY` has a breadcrumb. Each cell also has a separate diagnostic trigger, `S1-CT` to `S3-CT`, whose `ENTERED`, `LEFT`, `KILLED`, `FINISHED` and `FINISHED ALIVE` relays are traced. In particular, **LEFT remains observable after KILLED**. The diagnostic triggers do not feed the counts. A late FINISHED breadcrumb is expected and must not cause DOWN or EMPTY.

Its flown builder, indexes, links and serialized files stay identical; the existing byte comparison still checks its frozen artifacts. Production export and 2S explicitly select `KilledAndFinishedAlive`. Frozen inputs and flown files are untouched.

`flight_2s_predictions` uses `WalkerConfig::flown()`, both target orders and both same-tick delivery models. Positions are sampled once per second. Damage does not terminate a scripted track automatically, so the input ends the victim one 0.05 s tick after its sampled gate entry. A coalition-only synthetic track (not a generated aircraft) holds S2's CLEAR until the observed death-detection lag has elapsed. The 3.06 and 15.06 s game lags are represented as 3.05 and 15.05 s at 20 Hz.

## Reading and pass/fail

- **S1 passes** only if Tacview shows the second Yak alive and travelling outward at the kill about 300 m inside, its wreck crosses outside, KILLED steps 2 to 1 exactly once, and no LEFT or additional DOWN follows. EMPTY belongs only to the holder's later Delete. **Any LEFT from the wreck fails 0053:** supersede the decision before merge. If the wreck does not cross the ring, the cell is inconclusive and must be redesigned/reflown under a new flight name.
- **S2 passes** only if all four initial entries made it busy, A-C were removed, D died inside, CLEAR fired, and E entered **after CLEAR and before D's FINISHED**. No DOWN or EMPTY may follow that wreck event; E's Delete must empty it. An entry outside that window makes this cell inconclusive. Use raw events and Tacview to establish the actual ordering, not the ideal mission seconds.
- **S3 passes** if the first Delete raises 62 and 63, causes exactly one DOWN 2 and no EMPTY, and the holder's later Delete causes DOWN 1 and EMPTY. Any extra step or early EMPTY fails.
- **All cells:** check for removals in one cell causing steps in another, missed entries, natural departures, unexpected combat or losses. These invalidate the expected count. Keep the raw event times, count path, logs and Tacview together for Claude's review.

Not tested: a human quitting in flight, simultaneous entries/removals, and the changes proposed in 0054/0055. No new game facts are claimed until the flight is read.
