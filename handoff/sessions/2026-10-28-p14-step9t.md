## What was built

- **Trace build** (header checkbox beside Generate, every tab; `shell::page_header` gains a `beside_primary` slot).
  - The one hook is `trace_generated` in `ui.rs`. It is the only `instrument(` call in the UI, and it returns at once while the box is off.
  - It traces `decision_points()` across the whole file. On the Map and Air Tasking tabs it adds `airtask::trace_role_indexes`, which holds the A1 build's role list, now shared with the A1 test.
  - The `.trace.json` is written right after the `.Group` by `write_trace` (inside `save_with_sidecars`; Fighter and Map call it directly), so a language-file failure does not skip it.
  - The status adds " Trace build: N breadcrumbs."
  - Hooked on all eight header Generate paths. Off at start, never saved.
- **Replay…** (header button, every tab) opens `src/ui/replay.rs`.
  - It opens in its own OS window, like Help. Where viewports are embedded (the tests), it is an `egui::Window` placed below the header.
  - Picks: the `.Group`, an optional **Trace file…** (otherwise the sidecar next to the `.Group`), and the log folder (`missionlog::find_log_files`).
  - Readiness: a `.Group` and at least one log file. A missing sidecar is an orange note. A sidecar that does not match the `.Group` is refused (`trace::check_sidecar`).
  - Tabs with counts: Dead links, Unobserved, Never fired, Fired, Never spawned, Timeline. The first four need a trace. The Timeline filters by group and has a Position column.
  - The rows come from `missionlog::report_rows`. `report_markdown` now builds on the same rows; its output is byte-identical to before on the A1 and synthetic logs (checked with the CLI).
  - A new pick clears the report and the Map layer. Esc closes the window; when it is embedded, Esc does nothing else.
  - "Help ›" opens Help › Troubleshooting, where USER_MANUAL.md has a new "Trace build and Replay" part.
- **Map Replay layer.** Once a replay is loaded, the legend chip shows a "Replay" checkbox, with Spawn and Kill symbols while it is on.
  - Spawn dots and kill crosses are drawn in the DPRK or NATO colour by the object's country (5xx or 6xx), grey when unknown.
  - Markers come from `Replay.markers`, one per top-level object (no parent id). The first spawn record with a non-zero position is used; the end-of-mission dump writes 0,0,0. Breadcrumb spawns are skipped. There is one kill marker per killed top-level object.
- **Core additions:**
  - `TimelineEvent.group_path`: a breadcrumb's source group; a spawn's group by unique name; kills, takeoffs, landings and removals by object id.
  - `MapMarker`, `group_label`, `within_group`, `report_rows`, `UNOBSERVED_NOTE`, `NEVER_SPAWNED_NOTE`.
- **Dead-code allows:** removed the one in `trace.rs` (no new warnings). `missionlog.rs` had none. The three `// P14 step 12` allows remain.

## Live run (2026-10-28, the user said the screen was free)

The debug build was driven with `tools/ui-live/drive.ps1` on this PC: 1920 × 1080 screen, 150 % scaling. The run used the A1 acceptance flight: `handoff/p14-probes/acceptance-a1/P14_Acceptance_A1.Group`, its sidecar, and the 221 log files in `src/testdata/missionlog/real_46`.

- **Header.** "Replay…" and "Trace build" sit beside Generate on every tab.
- **Replay window.** It opens as its own OS window.
  - The readiness chip goes from "2 to do" to "1 to do" to "Ready". The sidecar is found next to the `.Group`.
  - Run replay gives Dead links 0, Unobserved 14, Never fired 213, Fired 33, Never spawned 15 and Timeline 429. These equal the command-line report.
  - The group filter works: "… / Air Tasking DPRK Logic / Clock" shows 8 of 429 events, the slot opening and GO at 600 s.
- **Map.** After Close, the Map draws the spawns and kills at Kimpo: red DPRK spawns (the Il-10s), blue NATO spawns and kill crosses. Unticking Replay hides them. The status bar carries the replay summary.
- **Fixed during the run** (each now has a guard test that fails on the old code):
  1. **Window size.** The window was 960 × 720 logical, taller than the screen at 150 %, so rows past about 800 px were off-screen. It now opens at 900 × 600 near the top left.
  2. **Table width.** The long MCU paths made the first column so wide that "Upstream fired" and "Downstream" were out of view. Columns now take a share of the width and wrap (`replay_tables_stay_inside_the_window`).
  3. **Tall rows.** Three successors in A1 have 26 to 28 downstream MCUs (about 4,900 characters), so one row was 1,600 px tall.
     - The Downstream cell now shows short names (`ReportRows.unobserved[].downstream_short`, no group path): at most three, then "+N more", with the full list on hover.
     - Cells are sized by their content, so rows and their stripes line up.
     - The same guard checks that no cell is taller than 160 px.
  4. **Map legend.** The Replay checkbox and its Spawn and Kill symbols pushed "All layers ▾" under the zoom chip, out of reach. Replay now has its own chip just above the legend (`map_legend_fits_with_a_replay_at_minimum_width`, at 1280 × 800).
- **A flaky test, found in the full suite.** `map_replay_layer_draws_spawns` failed once. The cause: `draw_korea_map` returns before the legend and the markers until the map texture has decoded on its thread. The two new Map tests now call `wait_for_map()`, like the other Map tests.
- **`drive.ps1` gains `-Window <title>`.** It acts on another of the app's windows, such as `'IL-2 Group Generator — Replay'` or a `'Select Folder'` dialog. It finds the window among the process's visible top-level windows. The default is still the main window.

**Live-run traps:**
- `dialog.ps1` in a **folder** picker types the path and presses Enter, which only opens the folder. Then click "Select Folder" with `drive.ps1 -Window 'Select Folder' -Action click -X 681 -Y 619` (this PC, 150 %).

## Plain event text (after the live run, at the user's request)

Event text in the Timeline and in the command-line report used to show Rust's internal form, e.g. "coalition Some(0), type Some(0)" or "country None". It now shows the number, or "?" when the log line has none, as the ids already did.

This applies to objectives (coalition, type, result, icon), spawns (country) and player planes (country). Test: `replay_event_text_is_plain`.

On the A1 logs the report changes only in the 298 lines that held such a value. Older reports already in the repo (such as `handoff/P14-probe-replay.md`) keep the old wording.
