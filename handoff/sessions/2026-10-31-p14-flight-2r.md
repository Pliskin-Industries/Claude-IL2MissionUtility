# Flight 2R: does the player count per area count in the game? (no pilot)

**Flown 2026-10-31** (logs `real_58`): every cell as predicted. Results: `handoff/P14-probe-results.md`, "Flight 2R". Decision 0050, option D. Files: `handoff/p14-probes/flight2r/`. Code: `src/airtask/presence.rs` (the count) and `build_count_probe` in `src/airtask/probe.rs`.

## The count under test

`presence::build_side` gives each area 26 MCUs:
- one Complex Trigger on the other side's player country;
- a relay chain from 0 to 3, then busy;
- one Closer 0 `CLEAR` check zone;
- one `EMPTY` relay.

How it counts:
- **Up:** Entered (58).
- **Down:** Left (60) and Finished (62). A kill counts down when its wreck lands, about 15 s later (flight 2P).
- **Busy:** a fourth entry. While busy, leaves do not count. 2 s later `CLEAR` is armed; it clears busy when nobody of the watched side is left, AI included.
- **Each step comes on one tick after the old one goes off.** One event therefore moves one step, but an event in the same tick as a step change, or the next tick (about 0.05 to 0.1 s), is lost:
  - a lost entry leaves the count too low, so the area empties while a player is still inside;
  - a lost leave leaves it too high, which is safe.

Here each count is a DPRK-side count watching 601, with 2M's 601 Yak stand-ins as NATO players. The count circle is 6 km to keep cells small; the design default is 50 km.

## Reading

- **D works as built if:**
  - R1, R2 and R4 empty exactly where predicted;
  - R4 shows no DOWN while busy, and clears twice;
  - each entry moves the count one step (UP k once per plane);
  - no cell steps on another cell's removals.
- **R3 shows the close-together risk** on the safe side: a pair deleted at once leaves the count high. Two entries within 0.05 to 0.1 s of each other would leave it low; for two players at 500 km/h that is 7 to 14 m apart along the track at the ring. So is an entry that close to another player's leave.
- **Not tested here:** what a player who quits in flight raises (flight 2N).
