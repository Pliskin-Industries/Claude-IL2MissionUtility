4. **Step 11 item 5:** the mission's load time and the server's tick rate on a real dedicated server.

### Choices, and why

- **Two pairs on both sides, not two pilots on one side** (decision 0059). With four pilots, each side's area gets its own pair, and the NATO pair covers what no flight has: a NATO player starting a DPRK sortie.
- **85 km between the areas, and each pair leaves away from the other** (the DPRK pair north, the NATO pair south). Neither pair's aircraft can enter the other's circles, so the two halves read independently.
- **Pilot 2 spawns 4 minutes after pilot 1,** so pilot 1 is alone in the area for 4 minutes first. One air start per side, so nobody spawns on top of anyone.
- **Il-10s for the DPRK sortie, not the Yak raid,** and F-80 HVAR strikes for NATO: neither has air-to-air orders.
- **The NATO pair flies at 3,000 m and pilot 2 of each pair 500 m above pilot 1:** clear of the Il-10s' rear gunners (1,500 m), and two pilots on one orbit never share a height.
- **Leave at S + 11 and S + 14:** the copy must have arrived (the count can end it only after arrival) and must still be well inside its 600 s on station, so `EMPTY` ends it, not `COLD`.
