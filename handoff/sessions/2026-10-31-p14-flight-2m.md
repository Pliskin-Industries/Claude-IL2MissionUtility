**Reading:**
- `MBn-Z0 fired` at the "last to go" time in every cell means ALL-out: 0048 keeps the check zone.
- At the "first to go" time, in any cell, it means ANY-out: 0048's options come back to the user.
- The walker models ALL-out.
- **Left rows** (0047's test): a `MBn-CT left` line at the time of:
  - MB1's or MB4's Delete means a Delete raises Left;
  - MB2's kill means a death raises Left;
  - MB3's line at 596 is the control (a fly-out raises Left, flights 1N and 1D).
- No Left at a Delete or a kill means a dead or removed plane stays counted, and 0047's safety net goes back to the user.
