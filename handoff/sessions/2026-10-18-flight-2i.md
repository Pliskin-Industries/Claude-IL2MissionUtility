## What to measure

## Reading

- **The rule holds** if, in three runs: no losses in any cell; the Yak pairs keep every pass above 20 m (as 2F's PL and 2G's GX); the F-51's covering pair does not cross its lead in the transit weave (as 2H's HS); the F-51's attack pair at Loose stays at or above 2H's figures (12.8–25.3 m).
- **A failure** goes to the user before the branch is merged, with where it happened (reversal, weave or steady turn).
