## What was built

- **`airtask::move_map_ai_off_player_countries(root)`** (`src/airtask/export.rs`, exposed from `mod.rs`). It is one recursive pass over the finished base map. It sets `Country` 601 to 602 and 501 to 503 on every `Plane`, with three exceptions:
  - it does not descend into an `Airfield` (player spawns);
  - it skips a plane whose `CoopStart` is not 0 (a co-op player slot);
  - it leaves every other block and every other country alone.
- **`threshold::ai_country_for_player(i32)`** holds the player to AI country map, next to the existing country constants.
- **One call in `generate_front_file`** (`src/ui/map.rs`), right after `stamp_missions` and only when `build_air_packs` returned packs (`with_air`). Missions without Air Tasking, or with the plan unticked or with no valid entry, take the old path unchanged.
- **USER_MANUAL.md**: one sentence in the Air Tasking paragraph on Map Generate. The spec (§6.1) asks for no UI text, so the app shows none.
- The planned "skip the Air Tasking group" rule was dropped while building: its planes are already 602 / 503 (decision 0008), and its Complex Triggers are not planes. The UI test checks the triggers still watch 601 and 501.

## Generate output that changes (missions with Air Tasking only)

Only the `Country` line of `Plane` blocks with `CoopStart = 0` outside an `Airfield` changes:
- in the fighter pack groups, every plane: 601 → 602 (NATO), 501 → 503 (DPRK);
- in strike mission groups, loaded army groups and reference groups, each such plane on 601 or 501.

Nothing else changes: no MCU or block is added or removed, and indexes, links, positions, ground units, ships, airfields, air starts, co-op player planes, and every trigger's country filter (including Air Tasking's `THsaa TRIGGER`) stay as they were.
