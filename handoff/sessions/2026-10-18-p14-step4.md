## The cell (default `PresenceCheckImpl::TriggerReactivate`)

Names follow plan §3.0 (`THsaa`, `s` = N or D, `aa` = 1-based area number per side). r_in = R × inner ratio (6 km), D = dwell (60 s), H = hold (120 s).

```
 THsaa TRIGGER (MCU_TR_ComplexTrigger, flown 1N shape, Radius r_in, Country = other side's players: N watches 501, D watches 601)
   OnEvent 58 (Object Entered) ─► THsaa ARM, THsaa SEEN                  <- live from mission load; takes no pulse
 THsaa ARM (latch relay) ─► THsaa ENTRY OFF (Deactivate: TRIGGER, ARM)
                        └► THsaa DWELL (timer D)

 CHECK (event): THsaa DWELL and THsaa HOLD each target these two
   ├─► THsaa CHECK ON (Activate: SEEN, CLOSE DELAY, TRIGGER)             ⟲ re-arm; SEEN and CLOSE DELAY listed before TRIGGER
   └─► THsaa CLOSE (timer 2 s)
         ├─► THsaa CLOSE OFF (Deactivate: TRIGGER, SEEN)                  <- first the check is closed
         └─► THsaa CLOSE DELAY (timer 0.05 s)                             <- FAIL: its output, 0.05 s later

 PASS = THsaa SEEN (relay; inactive except during a check) targets
   ├─► THsaa PASS OFF (Deactivate: CLOSE DELAY, TRIGGER, SEEN)            <- cancels the running timeout (flight 0 J1); latch
   ├─► THsaa HOT ON (Activate: hot_consumers[area])
   ├─► THsaa COLD OFF DELAY (timer 0.05 s) ─► THsaa COLD OFF (Deactivate: cold_consumers[area])
   ├─► THsaa HOLD (timer H)
   └─► pass_subscribers[area] (ZNii ARMED)

 FAIL = THsaa CLOSE DELAY targets
   ├─► THsaa COLD ON (Activate: cold_consumers[area])
   ├─► THsaa HOT OFF DELAY (timer 0.05 s) ─► THsaa HOT OFF (Deactivate: hot_consumers[area])
   └─► THsaa REARM ON (Activate: ARM, TRIGGER)                            ⟲ re-arm
```

- **Phases.** Entry: TRIGGER and ARM on, SEEN off, so an Entered reaches ARM only. Check: TRIGGER, SEEN and CLOSE DELAY on, ARM off, so an Entered reaches SEEN only.
- **Start states:** `SideLogic.init_off` gets SEEN and the HOT set (COLD on, HOT off). Nothing is added to `mission_begin`.
- **Pass and timeout in one tick** (third review T9): the timeout counts 0.05 s after `CLOSE OFF`; a pass in that tick deactivates `CLOSE DELAY`, which cancels it (DepthFirst: before it starts or while it runs; Batched: the deactivation commits at tick end, before the 0.05 s expiry).
- **18 MCUs, 1 Complex Trigger, 0 check zones** per (side, area). Plan §3.5: 23 MCUs, 2 check zones.

## Fallback (`PresenceCheckImpl::ZonePulse`)

TRIGGER does the entry only (OnEvent 58 → ARM). The check is `THsaa INNER` (MCU_CheckZone, Closer 1, `Zone` = r_in, the other side's coalition) with `THsaa INNER PULSE` (0.1 s): CHECK ON activates CLOSE DELAY and INNER; CHECK also pulses INNER PULSE → INNER; INNER is the PASS event (no SEEN); CLOSE OFF and PASS OFF deactivate INNER instead of TRIGGER and SEEN. **19 MCUs, 1 Complex Trigger, 1 check zone.** Enemy AI inside r_in at a check passes.

## Builder
