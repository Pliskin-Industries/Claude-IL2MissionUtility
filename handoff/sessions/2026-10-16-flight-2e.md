# Flight 2E design: does Safe density hold for Pairs and the propeller built-ins? (2026-10-16)

Branch `claude/p14-flight-2e`, cut from `claude/formation-safe-0019` (`29405cf`, which holds decision 0019's build `077e14b`). Probe prompt: `handoff/P14-flight-2e-codex-prompt.md`.

## Why

Decision 0019 (`docs/decisions/0019-air-formations-fly-at-safe-density.md`) puts every AI air formation at `FormationDensity = 1` (Safe). The evidence is flight 2D: F-80C Wedges only. Two kinds of formation the app ships were not flown at Safe:

- **Pairs** (formation 19): the Yak airfield raid and the F-51 ArmedRecon. In flight 2D, Pairs at Dense flew closest of all (26 aircraft-seconds under 20 m in a run).
- **The propeller types**: F-51D, Yak-9P, Il-10. They fly slower and lower than the F-80C.

## The idea: fly the built-ins exactly as the app ships them

After decision 0019 the built-in files already carry density 1, and Air Tasking's placement sets density 1 on every Formation command anyway (`place_air_sortie`, option A). So flight 2E changes **nothing** in the sorties: each cell is a built-in file, parsed, placed by `place_air_sortie` and wrapped by the shell, the same path flight 2D used. This tests what users get, and needs no probe edit of any sortie.

- **NATO and DPRK are enemies.** The NATO and DPRK cells are kept far apart. Every sortie spawns about 300 s of flight south of its target (heading 0) and flies straight north, so each path is a north–south line at its target's z. Each NATO path is at least 90 km from each DPRK path (EA–EY 90 km, EC–EY about 100 km, EA–EI and EC–EI about 135 km), far beyond Zone Out (35 km) and visual range. EY and EI (both DPRK) are 45 km apart; EA and EC (both NATO) lie on one line, 47 km from EC's target to EA's spawn.
- **Stagger.** Starts 60 s apart with count cues (4, 8, 16, 20 aircraft), as in flight 2D, so any overload message can be tied to a count.

No transit has been measured for these types. Placement spawns each sortie at the WP 1 speed × 300 s from its target, so `WP 1` is predicted at START + 300 s (the F-80C took 286 s). The first run measures the real times from the breadcrumbs.

The Yak raid's short attack is how it ships; the probe does not lengthen it. Its observation window is transit, descent and 300 s on station.

## What to measure (three runs, Tacview)

- **Losses**: every aircraft destroyed, with the attacker (`AType:3`), Tacview height above ground (`AGL`) and the nearest aircraft 0.5 s before. A low `AGL` loss is a ground impact, not a formation collision.
- **Spacing**: nearest aircraft of the **same flight** (Tacview `Name` ends in Red 1x or Blue 2x, and the type tells the cells apart), per phase (transit, descent, on station), and aircraft-seconds under 20 m. Also, separately, the nearest aircraft of the **other flight** in the same cell: EY and EI send two flights to one shared WP 1 at one altitude, and flight 2D did not test that. A close pass between flights is a different finding from decision 0019.
- **Overload**: the first time the overload message shows, against the count cues.
- **No combat**: every live aircraft at 15:00 still has its full ammunition (`BUL`).

**Reading.** Safe holds for these formations if no same-flight pair comes under 20 m in three runs and nothing is lost to a collision. A Pairs flight closer than flight 2D's Safe Wedge (27 m closest) would be reported against decision 0019.

**Analysis tool.** `tools/p14/flight2d_spacing.py` groups by activation time only. After the flight, a `flight2e_spacing.py` will group by Tacview `Name` (type and flight) and add the other-flight distance. Not part of the probe build.

## Code (probe only)

`src/airtask/probe.rs`: `Flight::TwoE`, its run sheet, cues and writer, using the flight 2D path. Two small changes to shared code: the empty-target rows gain a "built-in as shipped" variant, and `add_sortie_probe_source` and `close_sortie_probe` take the side from the inspected sortie instead of always NATO. Every earlier flight is NATO, so its output stays the same byte for byte (2B, 2C and 2D are frozen anyway). No product code changes and no product MCUs are added.
