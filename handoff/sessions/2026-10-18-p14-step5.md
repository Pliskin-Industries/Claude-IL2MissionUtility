## Does §3.7 still hold after steps 3 and 4?

Yes, at its boundaries. Checked against the code:

- **Copy pointer:** `assemble_side` builds `ENii COPY r` (relays, r ≥ 2 in `init_off`), `STARTED ON` / `STARTED OFF` links and one `ENii EXHAUSTED` per entry, and returns them in `SideCopies.entries` (`EntryCopies { number, area, mode, exhausted, copies }`). The clock adds only `GO` → every `COPY r`, and pushes `START` → `ENii LAST SET` and `EXHAUSTED` → its own relays to `SideLogic.links`.
- **Activate-mode copies (0017):** one stamped copy per run; nothing in the clock touches a copy.
- **HOT / COLD pairs:** the clock registers `ENii HOT` / `ENii COLD` in `hot_consumers` / `cold_consumers` under `EntryCopies.area`; `threshold::build_side` (run after it) switches them and puts the HOT relays in `init_off` (it already handles clock pairs, `threshold.rs` ≈190).
- **U24 timing** (D = 60 s, H = 120 s): only how long an area stays HOT; the clock reads the state and is unchanged. An area is COLD from mission start until a player has loitered about 60 s inside r_in, so with no player anywhere the clock launches nothing (U6, D24).
- **Not done by step 3:** the D15 shuffle. `assemble_side` numbers entries in pool order; the clock shuffles its own waterfall order from `AirPlan.seed`.

What changes is only the inside of the clock: flight 0 settled the behaviours §3.7 hedged against. Decision 0029 proposes a leaner build (B) on those facts.

## The cell as proposed (0029 B; names per §3.0, s = N or D)

```
 Translator Mission Begin ─► CLK s FIRST (timer, first slot) ─► CLK s TICK (relay)
 CLK s TICK ─► CLK s PERIOD (timer P) ─► CLK s TICK                                    (loop)
           ├► CLK s SLOT ON (Activate: SLOT OPEN)                                       ⟲ re-arm, 0.1 s before any jitter
           └► CLK s JITTER k (k = 1..J, Time 0.1 + (k−1)·step, Random p_k) ─► CLK s SLOT OPEN
 CLK s SLOT OPEN (latch relay)
   ├─► CLK s SLOT OPEN OFF (Deactivate: SLOT OPEN)
   ├─► every ENii AVAILABLE (relay; off once exhausted) ─► ENii RANDOM ON (Activate ENii RANDOM)
   ├─► CLK s NO REPEAT (0.1 s) ─► every ENii LAST (relay) ─► ENii EXCLUDE (Deactivate ENii RANDOM)
   ├─► CLK s REROLL ON (Activate: REROLL, ATTEMPT END)                                  ⟲ re-arm
   ├─► CLK s SLOT CLOSE (timer = slot window) ─► CLK s REROLL OFF (Deactivate: REROLL)
   └─► CLK s DRAW (0.3 s) ─► CLK s LAST CLEAR (Deactivate every ENii LAST), INPUT, ATTEMPT END
 CLK s Randomizer:INPUT (relay) ─► CLK s ReOpen Outputs (Activate every ENii OUT)
                                └► ENii RANDOM (0.5·k s, p% by weight, k = waterfall position) ─► ENii OUT ─► ENii HOT, ENii COLD
                                                                                             └► ENii CLOSE (Deactivate later ENxx OUT; not for the last)
 CLK s ATTEMPT END (timer A = L + 0.5) ─► CLK s REROLL (relay) ─► INPUT, ATTEMPT END
 ENii HOT  (in the area's HOT set)  ─► ENii GO (relay) ─► CLK s WIN (Deactivate: ATTEMPT END, REROLL), every ENii COPY r (last run first)
 ENii COLD (in the area's COLD set) ─► CLK s AREA aa OUT (Deactivate every ENxx RANDOM on area aa)
 SNjj START (every copy of entry ii) ─► ENii LAST SET (Activate ENii LAST)                         (SideLogic.links)
 ENii EXHAUSTED ─► ENii AVAILABLE, ENii RANDOM, ENii GO                                           (SideLogic.links)
```

- p_k = `weighted_random_pct([100−q; J] ++ [q·J])[k]` (q = quiet %): 27 / 36 / 57 % at the defaults; about 20 % of slots stay quiet. Entry `RANDOM` % = `weighted_random_pct` of the weights in shuffled order.
- L = 0.5·(n+1), A = L + 0.5, slot window = min(300, (a + 10)·A): unchanged from §3.7 (n clock-eligible entries, a distinct areas among them).
- Start states (`init_off`): every `ENii LAST`, `CLK s REROLL`. `mission_begin`: `CLK s FIRST`.
- `Schedule.enabled = false`, or no `Clock` / `Both` entry on the side: no `CLK` or `EN` clock MCU at all.
