# Flight 2G design: can a setting or an MCU stop the wingman crossing its lead? (2026-10-17)

Branch `claude/p14-flight-2g-be3801`, cut from `claude/p14-flight-2f` at `76b432e`. Probe prompt: `handoff/P14-flight-2g-codex-prompt.md`.

## Why

Flights 2C to 2F traced every AI formation collision to one manoeuvre: in a turn, an aircraft crosses its leader's track nearly abeam, at almost the same height, and does not avoid it (`handoff/P14-probe-results.md`, "Flight 2F"). The user ruled this a game bug (decision 0021): what the app writes for it is a workaround, a value change beats an added MCU, and the workarounds are listed to remove when the game is fixed. Decision 0020 (Proposed) recommends Pairs at Loose, which kept every pass above 20 m in flight 2F but spreads the pair about 200 m.

Flight 2G asks whether something else stops the crossing while the shipped formation (Pairs at Safe) stays: the AI's skill, a formation change only for the attack, or the geometry of the arrival. The user agreed to test AI skill and a formation change, and asked about turn geometry.

## The frame: flight 2F's, five cells

Every cell is the built-in `1950_DPRK_Yak_AirfieldRaid_2plus2.Group` (S4: two pairs sharing one `WP 1`, 4 Yak-9P, DPRK, AILevel 2), parsed, placed by `place_air_sortie` and wrapped by the shell as in flight 2F, Pairs at Safe as shipped. Each cell changes one thing on the placed copy, after placement. Raids start 330 s apart on targets A and B used in turn, so **at most 8 aircraft fly at once** (2F: mission time 1.00× wall time). ### What each cell tests

- **GC, control.** In 2E and 2F the Yak Blue pair came within 10 m at `WP 1` + 12–13 s in all six runs and collided in three. GC is 2F's PS on the same target B, with only the START, prefix and ids different. If GC does not repeat the close pass, the other cells cannot be read.
- **G3, G4: does a better AI pilot avoid its lead?** A value change on four Planes, no MCUs. If it works it is the cheapest fix, but it is not free: AILevel is also combat skill, so every DPRK and NATO AI flight would fight harder (a gameplay change, the user's call).
- **GX: Loose only for the attack.** The transit stays at Safe (the shipped look); on arrival at `WP 1` each lead is told Pairs at Loose, 0.5 s before its AttackArea order. Does a wider slot during the orbit stop the crossing, and does the slot change itself, which starts as the lead reverses, cause one?
- **GA: no turn reversal on arrival.** Placement puts `WP 1` and the AttackArea centre on the same point, so the lead arrives over the centre of its orbit and reverses (60° left for about 5 s, then a steady 60° right orbit); the wingman, now inside, crosses. With the centre 3 km ahead (`WP 1` 1.5 km outside the 1.5 km circle, about 11 s of straight flight to its edge), the lead should enter the orbit without reversing. If the crossover goes away, the fix is a placement change with no MCUs. It addresses only the reversal kind of collision (4 of 9 resolved), not the steady-turn kind (5 of 9).

### Refinements to the proposal, and why

1. **GX has no switch back to Safe at `DONE HUB`.** The shell deletes every aircraft 60.6 s after `DONE HUB` (`DONE HUB` → end timer → `DELAYED END ORDERS` 60 s → `DELETE DELAY` 0.5 s → `DELETE`, `src/airtask/shell.rs`; 2F measured 60.6 s). A switch back would buy one minute of tighter formation before the delete, cost a second MCU per flight, and add a slot change during the turn for home, where flight 2F's PL had its only other close pass (12.3 m, two different pairs). So GX is **one MCU per flight, two per raid**, the fewest a formation change can cost. If the user wants the switch back for a product with a longer return leg, it is a second flight.
2. **The Loose order fires from `WP 1` itself, not from the attack timers.** The waypoint already exists and already fires the attack timers 0.5 s later, so the formation order reaches the lead before the AttackArea order and cannot interrupt it.
3. **The new Formation commands copy the lead's own command** (`FlightSize = 1`, `WaitForWingmen = 1`), changing only `FormationDensity`, `Name`, `Index` and position, so the change is exactly "the same order, Loose". Risk: with `WaitForWingmen = 1` a mid-flight order might make the lead slow down for its wingman; Tacview's lead speed will show it.
4. **GC flies target B**, as 2F's PS did, so the control is the cell that collided at 1.00× on the same ground.
5. **GA's offset is along the approach heading** (placement flies every raid in +X, heading 0), so `WP 1` timing does not change, nor do `ON STATION`, `DONE HUB` or `DELETE DELAY`.
6. **No cell for the steady-turn kind** beyond G3 and G4: no setting is known to touch it, and 2G's crossing count over the whole orbit (below) measures it in every cell.

## What to measure (three runs, Tacview)

`tools/p14/flight2f_spacing.py` and `flight2f_crossover.py`, adapted to five cells (`flight2g_spacing.py`, `flight2g_crossover.py`):

- **Server speed** per run (log modification times), **losses** (attacker, height, nearest aircraft), **attitude-jump episodes** with the nearest aircraft.
- **Same-flight spacing**: median per phase, aircraft-seconds under 20 m, closest pass at Tacview's 0.2 s frames, with the time after `WP 1`.
- **Every crossing of the lead's track**, per pair, from `WP 1` − 5 s to `DONE HUB` (not only the first reversal): the wingman's slot before `WP 1` (behind, right, below), and at each crossing the time after `WP 1`, fore-aft and vertical offset and distance. Count crossings within 20 m.
- **The lead's path on arrival**: whether it reverses (left then right bank) and when; for GA, whether the reversal is gone. For GX, the lead's speed after `WP 1` and the wingman's slot once Loose is set.

## Reading

- A cell **works** if, in three runs, no same-flight pass came under 20 m and no crossing of the lead's track came within 20 m, while GC repeats its close pass (within 10 m at `WP 1` + 12–13 s).
- **G3 or G4 works:** the cheapest fix is a skill value, but it changes combat for every AI flight; the user decides whether that price is acceptable.
- **GX works:** two MCUs per two-flight raid keep the shipped look in transit. Compare with 0020's option A (Pairs at Loose throughout, no MCUs): GX is worth its MCUs only if a tight transit formation matters.
- **GA works:** the reversal crossover is a placement artefact; moving the area ahead removes it with no MCUs, for every formation. The steady-turn crossings remain and need their own answer (Loose, or skill).
- **Nothing works:** decision 0020's option A stands as the workaround, and 2G's crossings go into bug report 3 as more evidence.
- Three runs are few; the crossing clearances and their repeat at the same second are the stronger evidence, as in 2E and 2F.
