## Flight A4: a Yak copy and a DPRK-side extension

### The mission (as built; checked by `check_a4_build`)

- Map tab, default date (22 April 1951), no fighter pack.
- **One DPRK area, B,** at (110,000, 250,000): 2N's area B, about 32 km south of the front. It watches NATO players (country 601). Entry trigger 8 km (R 16 km), **N = 1**, dwell 80 s, hold 160 s. Player count circle 20 km.
- **One Zone entry, ZD01:** `1950_DPRK_Yak_AirfieldRaid_2plus2` (2 + 2 Yak-9P, guns; AttackArea ground targets, 1.5 km). Chance 100 %, cooldown 5 min. No clock entry, so no clock.
- **Three Yak copies, SD01 to SD03,** on 503 (a country the area does not count). Each spawns 30 km north-east of B at 3,050 m and flies to `WP 1` over B at 500 km/h.
- **Copy timers:** `ON STATION` 300 s (the raid's own 5 min) after arrival; then `COLD` ends it, or `HOT` gives **one `EXTEND` of 600 s**; `HARD STOP` 1,716 s after `START`; `DELETE DELAY` after `DONE HUB`.
- **One air start, "P14 A4 NATO air start",** on 601: an F-51D at **4,500 m**, heading 090, 22 km west and 2.5 km south of B (2N's NATO air start, 1,500 m higher). Outside the 20 km count circle.

### Design choices

- **The Yak raid as the only entry.** It is the one built-in that has never flown as an Air Tasking copy. A Zone entry with chance 100 % gives a certain start at the first HOT check.
- **4,500 m, 1,450 m above the Yaks.** The raid has no air-to-air orders, but the Yak-9P is a fighter. The extra height keeps the player out of their way. A shoot-down inside B would reset the count and spoil the extension.
- **Leave after the delete, not before.** The extension needs B HOT at the end of on-station time, so the player orbits until `DONE HUB` and the delete. Leaving then shows the count going down and the zone re-arming with no new start.

## Flight 2T: the quiet roll and the jitter steps, counted

### The mission (as built; checked by `check_t2_build`)

- Map tab, default date, no fighter pack, **no air start** (nobody flies).
- One NATO area at (190,000, 280,000) with a Clock entry (F-80 HVAR strike), one DPRK area at (110,000, 250,000) with a Clock entry (Il-10 attack). No area goes HOT, so nothing launches.
- **Both clocks** as shipped, except the times: quiet 20 %, 3 jitter steps; **first slot 60 s, period 60 s, jitter step 15 s.** `TICK` at 60, 120, … s on each side.
- **The roll:** at each `TICK`, `JITTER 1`, `2` and `3` start at +0.1, +15.1 and +30.1 s with Random 27, 36 and 57 %. The first that passes fires `SLOT OPEN`; the rest find it shut. All three fail with chance 0.73 × 0.64 × 0.43 = **0.20**: the slot is quiet.

### Design choices

- **A slot a minute, not 20 minutes.** The rate is a property of the game's Random roll, not of the period. 120 slots take an hour instead of 40.
- **Both sides.** Two independent samples per minute.
- **No pilot, no HOT area.** The clock still rolls (flight A0). With nothing HOT, no draw can launch, so the hour adds no aircraft and no load.

### Run steps

1. Delete any `P14_Probe_2T.msnbin` in `Dogfight`. Host from the client as for A4, `P14_Probe_2T` alone in the rotation.
2. **Do not spawn.** Stay on the briefing screen. Read the mission clock from the newest log file's last `T:` ((T − 3) / 50 s).
3. **Leave after mission time 3,640 s** (60:40): Esc, leave the server (results screen within about 15 s; `CloseMainWindow` if it loops).
4. Copy the logs with `cp -p` to `src/testdata/missionlog/real_62/`.

### Reading it

`TICK` has no breadcrumb; its times are exact (60·k s). For each side and each `TICK` from 60 to 3,600 s (60 per side, **120 in all**):
- **quiet** if no `SLOT OPEN` (DPRK 5000, NATO 5310) comes before the next `TICK`;
- otherwise its **step** is `SLOT OPEN` − `TICK`: 0.1, 15.1 or 30.1 s.

Each `JITTER k` breadcrumb (DPRK 5080 / 5090 / 5100, NATO 5390 / 5400 / 5410) logs when that timer passes, so each timer's own pass rate is counted as well.
